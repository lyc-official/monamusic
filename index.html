What a lesson covers

  • Reading music

    Notes, rhythm and the signs on the page.

  • Technique

    Posture and hand shape for even, comfortable playing.

  • Listening

    Hearing the sound you make, and shaping it.

  • Repertoire

    Pieces matched to each student's level and taste.

Meet Mona

Mona Lim is an educator who teaches music and English.

She also performs on piano and keyboard, accompanies other musicians, and directs, choreographs and conducts choirs. Her most recent opera was Stravinsky's The Rake's Progress with New Opera Singapore.
